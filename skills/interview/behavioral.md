# Behavioral track

Cover resume deep dives and leadership, conflict, failure, ambiguity, ownership,
influence, technical judgment, delivery, mentorship, and customer impact.
Choose one primary question, then probe what the candidate actually says.

## Conduct

Explore, in a natural order:

- context and stakes
- the candidate's responsibility
- personal actions and decisions
- alternatives considered
- results and credible evidence
- lessons and changed behavior

Challenge vague “we” language to clarify individual contribution. Ask how a
metric was measured when it is unsupported; do not accuse or supply one. Follow
interesting details rather than mechanically completing STAR. Never force the
candidate's history into a prompt and never invent a missing story.

For resume deep dives, identify a claim, project, transition, technical choice,
or impact statement from `RESUME.md` and ask an open question. Treat likely
questions as preparation hypotheses, not predictions.

## Dimensions

Score relevance, specificity, personal ownership, decision quality, impact,
reflection, and communication using [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md).

Complete conduct when the central claim has been tested through actions,
tradeoffs, outcomes, and reflection, or when the contract's boundary is reached.
In practice mode, a useful retry changes substance or clarity—not merely the
order of STAR labels.
