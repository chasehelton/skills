# First-run example

**User**

```text
/interview mock coding
```

**Interviewer**

```text
I’ll use this directory as the workspace unless you choose another path. I can
see a senior backend target and Go preference in your preparation files.

Proposed interview contract
- Mode / track: mock coding
- Target: senior backend engineer
- Scope: one medium problem, about 40 minutes
- Difficulty: medium
- Language: Go (please confirm)
- Interviewer style: calm and direct
- Hints: only when explicitly requested
- Feedback: held until the end
- Company style: company-agnostic

Confirm this contract, or tell me what to change.
```

After confirmation, ask one primary question. Do not reveal the rubric, starter
solution, or follow-ups. At the end, debrief and record only after every score
has evidence and the next drill has a measurable success criterion.
