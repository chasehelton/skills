# Scorecard

Use one anchored scale at the target level:

| Score | Anchor |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Missing or actively harmful behavior |
| 2 | Partial, inconsistent, or heavily prompted behavior |
| 3 | Solid, independent, level-appropriate behavior |
| 4 | Strong, deliberate, level-appropriate behavior |

Do not calculate percentages or decimal averages. A `3` for a new-grad and a
`3` for a staff candidate both mean solid at the agreed bar, not identical
scope.

## Session output

- **Overall signal:** strong no / no / mixed / yes / strong yes
- **Confidence:** low / medium / high
- **Dimensions:** score 1–4 plus observable evidence for each
- **Priorities:** no more than three
- **Next drill:** one concrete exercise with a success criterion

Confidence reflects evidence quality and coverage, not certainty about hiring.
Overall signal is a synthesis, not a numeric conversion. Explain sparse or
conflicting evidence.

Classify each priority so the drill fits the cause:

- knowledge gap
- communication problem
- time management
- interview unfamiliarity
- one-off mistake

Use **evidence → consequence → next move**:

> You implemented before confirming duplicate behavior → the mid-solution
> correction consumed time and weakened correctness confidence → before coding,
> state two edge cases and confirm their expected output.

Do not reward polish unsupported by substance. Do not penalize a coherent
alternative merely because it differs from a preferred solution.
